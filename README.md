# Adam and the Answerable Being

**Adam and the Answerable Being** is a standalone work by Syed Raheel Shahzad.

The book explores Qur’anic anthropology, human origin, dignity, fitrah, trust, guidance, repentance, moral responsibility, and answerable humanity.

It examines what makes the human being more than biological life: the creature addressed, taught, tested, guided, forgiven, and held answerable before Allah.

Repository:  
https://github.com/SyedRaheelShahzad/adam-and-the-answerable-being

Official cover image:  
https://syedraheelshahzad.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Adam-and-Answerable-human-being-Syed-Raheel-Shahzad.jpg

---

## Core Frame

The book asks:

**When does a creature become answerable?**

It distinguishes between biological description and moral anthropology. Biology may describe the organism, but revelation defines the answerable human.

The central argument is that the Qur’an presents Adam not merely as a biological specimen, but as the first revealed answerable human: addressed by Allah, taught the names, commanded, tested, fallen, forgiven, guided, and placed at the beginning of moral humanity.
